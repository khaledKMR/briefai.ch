How it works

3 steps to your answer

No account needed. Simply scan the letter, choose your language, understand.

Step 01

Photograph or paste your letter

Take a photo of your letter with the camera or paste the text. Text recognition runs locally on your device.

Step 02

Automatic anonymization

AHV numbers, IBANs and phone numbers are automatically replaced before any transfer — only anonymized text leaves your device.

Step 03

Analysis in your language

BriefAI explains what the letter means, what you need to do, and whether there is a deadline — in your chosen language.
